## Scope and mode

New-writing index and article pages. Visitor mode: Read.

## Audience, job, action, proof, and constraints

Readers need a clearly current body of writing distinct from the legacy archive. The first release contains four authored articles: the new Mcd79 introduction, the value of Copilot, a practical map of Copilot agents, and the need for an AI Productivity Framework. The primary action is choosing an article; factual product claims link to current Microsoft sources. Pages must export statically.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

The index is a dispatch board: one oversized white lead sheet and three bright, ruled supporting blocks. Articles become long-form instruction sheets with a saturated opening field, a yellow provenance rail, generous reading measure, and a ruled continuation strip.

## Unresolved decisions

Future publishing workflow, article imagery, taxonomy, and subsequent article inventory remain open.
