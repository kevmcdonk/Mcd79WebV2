## Scope and mode

Legacy archive index and preserved article pages. Visitor mode: Read.

## Audience, job, action, proof, and constraints

Readers need a clearly preserved body of earlier Mcd79 work with its original date, context, complete article content, and source provenance intact. This migration contains the ten most recent available posts, ordered newest first. The primary action is choosing an archive entry. Pages must export statically and remote legacy assets must remain bounded and responsive.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

The archive is a ruled inventory ledger rather than a second copy of the current blog. Oversized sequence numbers, compact dates, alternating working-blue fields, and long editorial titles create a visible historical run. Article pages use a paper opening and a yellow year/provenance rail.

## Unresolved decisions

Later migration batches, local asset preservation, historic tag recovery, and redirects from every original URL remain open.
