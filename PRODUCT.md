# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with static export. The production output must be deployable as static files without a Node.js server, API routes, server actions, or runtime rendering.

## Users

The primary audience is people who want to learn from Kevin McDonnell under the Mcd79 brand. They are looking for practical guidance on using AI productively in their own work and across increasingly broad organisational contexts.

## Product Purpose

Mcd79 is Kevin McDonnell's knowledge hub. It brings together:

- a preserved legacy blog;
- a new blog focused on productive use of AI;
- an archive of past and present conference sessions; and
- the AI Productivity Framework.

Success means readers can discover Kevin's existing work, follow his current thinking, and apply the AI Productivity Framework to improve how they evaluate and adopt AI tools.

## Positioning

The AI Productivity Framework connects tool evaluation with sustained behaviour change. It addresses productivity at four distinct levels:

1. personal;
2. team;
3. department; and
4. organisation.

Rather than presenting generic tool tips, it should help readers assess the value of different tools and integrate useful tools into their flow of habit.

## Operating Context

Readers may enter through legacy articles, new AI-focused writing, conference-session material, or the framework itself. The site should support movement between those bodies of knowledge while keeping their purpose and provenance clear.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve a distinct area for the legacy blog.
- Provide a distinct area for the new blog.
- Present past and current conference sessions.
- Provide a dedicated area for the AI Productivity Framework.
- Structure the framework around personal, team, department, and organisation levels.
- Cover both tool-value assessment and integration into habitual workflows.
- Reuse the content from the existing Mcd79 site at https://www.mcd79.com.
- The implementation stack is not yet decided.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the product name and public identity **Mcd79**.
- Kevin McDonnell remains the authorial voice and source of the site's knowledge.
- Preserve the continuity and factual integrity of existing Mcd79 content during migration.

## Product Principles

1. **Turn knowledge into practice.** Help readers move from understanding a tool to using it consistently in real work.
2. **Scale the lens deliberately.** Treat personal, team, department, and organisation adoption as related but distinct challenges.
3. **Evaluate before adopting.** Give readers a way to assess value instead of chasing tools for novelty.
4. **Preserve earned knowledge.** Keep legacy articles and conference history accessible, accurate, and clearly connected to Kevin's current work.
5. **Teach from experience.** Maintain Kevin's practical, challenge-led approach rather than presenting generic AI advice.
