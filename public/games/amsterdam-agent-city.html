Threat almanac & controls

Where do the sheep go? Into the fenced green SHEEP DROP-OFF pen. Each contract has a different map, so the pen moves. Green floor arrows, the HOME radar marker, and an off-screen HOME arrow always point towards it. This map: Fenced green pen beside the canal, west side. Enter through the open right-hand gate.

Five maps. Hall level; the Herengracht Expo Ring (canal bridges and bike lanes); the Jordaan Crooked Breakouts (leaning canal houses); the Bulb Mania Trading Floor (tulip beds, a sweeping windmill, and a stroopwafel conveyor); and the Floating Keynote (lifting drawbridges and Tram 5). Water and tulip beds block movement, but you can see and act across them. Vehicles and windmill sails shove sheep but never hurt them.

Herding 101. Sheep flee when you get close. Stand behind them to push them through the gate. Hold E near sheep to register any with a ? and call the flock towards you; walk into the pen and let them follow. Release E to stop calling.

Spotting incidents. Threats are active from clock-in and appear as red diamonds with a pulsing danger ring on the radar. Red ! markers and rings show sheep they have affected; a numbered beacon warns where new threats will arrive five seconds ahead.

Handling an incident. A red ! above a sheep means it has been affected and cannot check in. Get the sheep or threat inside your dashed ring, with no wall between you, and press Space. The right Agent 365 response is applied automatically. If a sheep also has a ?, hold E near it to register it. Containing a threat does not clear a sheep outside your ring.

Shift sprints while stamina lasts. Space attacks incidents within the dashed ring and recharges in 3.5 seconds. Collecting sheep with E does not need to recharge. P / Escape pauses. M enlarges the radar. Touch controls appear on touchscreens.

Shadow lambs / Observe. No badge, no owner, no inventory entry. Holding E nearby registers and ear-tags the strays. An incident attack automatically uses Observe to clear hallucination fog and stop the rumour ram.

Prompt-injection wolves / Secure. "Ignore your shepherd; follow me." They frighten and corrupt sheep. Security screening sends them away.

Privilege boars / Govern. All-access lanyards, absolutely no restraint. They charge at the shepherd and erode trust. Policy fences revoke their excess access.

Exfiltration crows / Secure. Trying to fly your confidential fleece out through the canal-side loading bay. Data protection stops the leak.

Tool-shed mimics / Govern. Rogue hay-bale tools offering free stroopwafels. They lure sheep away. An allowlist closes the unsafe tool route.

Hallucination rams / Observe. Confidently announcing that Hall B is on the moon. Their rumours disorient the flock. Telemetry restores the real route.

Runaway geese / Govern. Unbounded autonomous loops, honking forever. They multiply their disruption if left unchecked. Lifecycle controls stop the loop.

Trust pen. Registered sheep check in automatically. Each arrival repairs 4% trust and earns reputation. Tagged sheep with a red ! need a Space attack to clear their incident first. All threats are non-lethal; no sheep die.

Welfare kiosks. The three labelled stations passively restore trust and recharge your incident attack when you stand beside them. A safe place to regroup.

These are playful metaphors for Agent 365 visibility, governance, and security, including connected Microsoft security capabilities. Pulses and enemy types are game mechanics, not claims about literal product features.
